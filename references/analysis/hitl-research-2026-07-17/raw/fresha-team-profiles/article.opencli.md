# Put your team in the spotlight with enriched profiles

Learn how to invite your team members to create professional profiles that boost your business’s credibility, and attract more clients.

## **What are team member professional profiles?**

Professional profiles on Fresha give your team members a dedicated space to showcase their work and connect directly with new and existing clients. Whether you're a business made up of hairstylists, beauty therapists, or fitness coaches, professional profiles highlight your team's skills and services, helping your business stand out in [**the Fresha marketplace**](https://www.fresha.com/).

**Professional profiles are be** **visible to clients throughout their booking journey** and even after their appointment. Clients are able to explore your team’s profiles in three key stages:

-   **When booking an appointment with you**
    To enrich the booking experience, clients can not only choose the professional providing their service but also **view their profile** to get a genuine sense of who will be treating them.
    ![Put your team in the spotlight with enriched profiles](images/img_002.jpg)

-   **In their appointment details**
    After selecting their professional and confirming the booking, clients can review their appointment details and browse the selected team member’s portfolio, giving them a preview of the style and quality they can expect.

    ![Put your team in the spotlight with enriched profiles](images/img_003.jpg)

-   **On your Fresha marketplace venue**
    When clients browse your Fresha marketplace venue, they can click on any of your bookable team members to open their professional profile.

    ![Put your team in the spotlight with enriched profiles](images/img_004.jpg)

Clients can tap the **heart icon** in a team member profile to add the professional to their favorites, making it easy to book with them again in the future. Clients can find their favorited professionals and venues in the [**Favorites**](https://www.fresha.com/favorites) tab of their Fresha account.

## **How this benefits your business**

Each team member’s professional profile is a powerful marketing tool that attracts and inspires clients. It gives your team the opportunity to promote both themselves and your business by highlighting their:

**Profile**
Showcase the person behind the service. Team members can share their story, interests, languages, and social media links, building trust and connection before clients even book.

**Services**
Put their expertise front and center. Each profile highlights the services they’re qualified to perform for your business, making it easy for clients to choose the right professional.

**Reviews**
Highlight the impact of glowing reviews. Every review and star rating a team member earns is proudly displayed, helping build credibility and encouraging bookings.

**Professional profile portfolio**
Bring talent to life with a visual gallery. [**Team members can upload photos of their work to create a lasting gallery**](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/personal-account/101698-set-up-your-professional-portfolio) that showcases their craft and inspires clients to book with confidence.

![Put your team in the spotlight with enriched profiles](images/img_005.jpg)

When your team members upload service images to their individual professional portfolios, you can choose to feature them in your Fresha marketplace venue page. This helps showcase the full range of talent at your business.

[**You can manage which images appear from your Online profile content settings**](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/calendar/257-manage-online-bookings-settings).

## **What to do next?**

**For your team:**

-   **Invite your team**
    Send invites to your [**team members to join your workspace**](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/team/276-add-team-members-to-your-workspace) so they can create their own professional profiles.
-   **Help them shine**
    Encourage [**team members to personalize their profiles**](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/personal-account/267-set-up-your-professional-profile) with a photo, bio, and interests.
-   **Showcase their skills**
    Motivate team members to upload photos of their best work and build eye-catching professional portfolios that highlight their talent. They can [**use our handy guide**](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/personal-account/232-tips-for-the-best-profile-image) to create a profile that stands out.

**For your business:**

-   **Polish your profile**
    Clients love to know who’s behind the business. Refresh [**your professional profile**](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/personal-account/267-set-up-your-professional-profile) by updating your photo, interests, and bio to make a great impression.
-   **Update your treatments**
    Keep your service menu clear, organized, and up to date. Use our [**guide to help you optimize your treatment list**](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/catalog/100657-best-practices-for-creating-your-service-menu) for maximum impact.
-   **Refresh your venue images**
    Upload fresh, high-quality photos to make your marketplace profile stand out. [**Use our photography guidelines**](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/online-profile/198-follow-marketplace-photography-guidelines) for tips on capturing your venue at its best.

We recommend [**updating each team member’s profile photo and job title**](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/team/583-knowledge-base-article-5). This means that even if they haven’t set up their professional profile, clients can still put a name to a face and a title to a person, helping your business make a great first impression.
