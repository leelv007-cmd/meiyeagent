# How do I add photos to a client's appointment? (PhorestGo Portfolio)

![Avatar](images/img_001.jpg)

**Paul K**

Last updated: March 3, 2026

### This article will show you how you can capture appointment photos, store them in the PhorestGo Portfolio, and share them on your preferred social media platform.

####  In this Article

-   [**Adding a Client Appointment Photo**](#addimage)
-   [**Permission to Share Online**](#permission)

### Adding a Client Appointment Photo

To add a client appointment photo, start by tapping on the appointment.

![](images/img_002.jpg)

Next, scroll down and tap **Add Photos**, then choose **Open Camera** to take a new photo or **Upload Image** to upload an image from your device.

#### Permission to Share Online

After adding a photo, you'll be prompted to confirm whether the client has given permission for it to be shared online. It is important to get your client's permission.

![](images/img_006.jpg)

This prompt appears only after adding a photo, with permissions applying to **all photos in that appointment**. Changing permissions later updates sharing for all photos in that appointment. Photos in different appointments for the same client can have different permissions.

If you choose **Yes, sharing is allowed**; a Share button will be displayed when viewing the image. If you choose **No**, this button will not be displayed for that image.

![](images/img_007.jpg)

#### Viewing and Editing the Photo

After you've added your images, they are attached to that appointment. Tap the image to view it. From there, you can change the before/after image tag, share the image, delete it, or use the editor to enhance it.

![2024-07-02\_12-16-05.gif](images/img_008.jpg)

To delete an image, you must have access to **Phorest Go (Mobile)** > **Clients** > **Client Photos** > **Edit Photos**. [**Click here to learn more about updating access permissions.**](https://support.phorest.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007972614)

* * *

### Viewing Your Portfolio

To access your portfolio, open the PhorestGo sidebar menu and then select **Portfolio**.

![](images/img_009.jpg)

Here, you'll find a collection of all client photos you have taken, grouped by the dates they were taken.

You can tap an image to **share** or **delete** it.

![](images/img_010.jpg)

To delete an image, you must have access to **Phorest Go (Mobile)** > **Clients** > **Client Photos** > **Edit Photos**. [**Click here to learn more about updating access permissions.**](https://support.phorest.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007972614)
